Patient on Taxotere who used Amma For patients receiving Taxotere (docetaxel), the common reassurance that chemotherapy-related hair loss is temporary may not hold true. Permanent hair loss, clinically known as persistent chemotherapy-induced alopecia (pCIA), is a real, well-documented risk. Scalp cooling is the only FDA-cleared intervention shown to help prevent it.
