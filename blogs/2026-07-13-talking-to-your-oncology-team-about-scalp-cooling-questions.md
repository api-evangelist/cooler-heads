Starting chemotherapy comes with an overwhelming number of decisions, and hair loss is often one of the last things patients feel equipped to bring up, even though it's one of the side effects they worry about most. If you're considering scalp cooling, having the right conversation with your oncology team early can make a real difference in your experience. Don't wait for your care team to bring it up; you're allowed to ask.
