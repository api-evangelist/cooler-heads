One of the most common questions we hear is some version of: "Will this actually work for my hair?" It's a smart question; hair type, texture, and even hairstyle habits can all play a role in scalp cooling success and comfort. Here's what patients with different hair types should know going in. Fine or Thin Hair Patients with fine or thin hair sometimes worry that they have "less to protect," but fine hair can respond very well to scalp cooling.
