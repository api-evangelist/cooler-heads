Hair loss is one of the most visible and emotionally challenging side effects of chemotherapy. For many patients, keeping their hair during treatment can help preserve identity, confidence, and a sense of normalcy. Scalp cooling offers a scientifically proven way to reduce this side effect and can give patients more control during treatment.
