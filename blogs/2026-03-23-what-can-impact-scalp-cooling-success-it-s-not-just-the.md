Scalp cooling has transformed the chemotherapy experience for many patients, but one of the most important conversations is expectation setting: scalp cooling is not magic. It is a clinically supported tool designed to help reduce hair loss, but results depend on far more than just the machine itself. Setting realistic expectations is critical.
